# 📚 Sources — Exo7 (free, in French)

The [SYLLABUS](./SYLLABUS.md) stays the same. Ignore its Pearson page numbers and use this table instead.
Exo7 is written by French university lecturers and is free and legal. Each chapter has:
- a **cours** (`ch_….pdf`): read this
- a **fiche d'exercices** (`fic….pdf`): exercises with corrections
- usually a **YouTube playlist** of the lecture

Main pages: [Première année](https://exo7.emath.fr/un.html) · [Deuxième année](https://exo7.emath.fr/deux.html).
The full **Algèbre** and **Analyse** books (all chapters in one PDF) are in the *Livres* section of the first page.

> 🇬🇧 **Bonus:** some linear algebra sheets also exist **in English** (`fic1006x`). Use them to check your understanding, but write your solutions in French.

## How to use a fiche
- Exercises are rated by difficulty (stars). Do the **easy ones first**, then at least one harder one per topic.
- Many exercises have an **indication** (hint) and a **correction** at the end. Try for 20 min before opening the hint, and 20 more before the correction.
- The ☐ checklists in the SYLLABUS remain your exit test for each week.
